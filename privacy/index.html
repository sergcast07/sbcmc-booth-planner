Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

The SBCMC app ("the app") is provided by the Southern Baptist Church Music Collective ("SBCMC," "we") for attendees and exhibitors of the SBCMC conference. You can use the app without creating an account.

What the app collects

  • Registration details. If you link your name tag in the app, the app retrieves your name and the sessions you registered for from your conference registration.
  • Booth visits. If an exhibitor scans your name tag at their booth, we record the visit and share your registration contact details (name, email address, phone number, job title and organization) with that exhibitor, so they can follow up with you. Only exhibitors whose booth you visit receive your details. If you don't want to share them, don't have your name tag scanned.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a notification token for your device and the list of sessions you've saved, so we can send schedule changes and reminders for those sessions.
  • Session feedback. Ratings and comments you submit are stored without your name.

What stays on your device

Your session notes, bookmarks and saved speakers and exhibitors are stored only on your device. Calendar events you add are written to your device's calendar.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. We don't collect your location.

Camera, calendar and photos

The camera is used only to scan name tag QR codes. Calendar access is used only when you choose to add sessions. Photo access is used only by conference coordinators uploading exhibitor logos.

How long we keep it

Conference data, including booth visit records and notification tokens, is deleted within 12 months after the conference.

Your choices

You can unlink your badge in the app at any time, turn off notifications in your device settings, or ask us to delete your information by emailing info@sbcmc.org.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@sbcmc.org
